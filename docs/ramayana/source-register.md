# Ramayana guides: source register and editorial scope

Updated: 24 September 2026.

## Scope

Eight evergreen reading guides: one overview and seven Kanda guides. Original prose summaries, interpretive discussion, continuous chapter-range maps, references and internal links. These are not a full Sanskrit text, a complete translation, or a verse-by-verse commentary. Each Kanda map accounts for its declared main numbered sequence; it does not assign a separate original summary to every individual sarga.

## Edition policy

Books 1–6 use the chapter arrangement on ValmikiRamayan.net: Desiraju Hanumanta Rao for Bala, Aranya and Kishkindha; K. M. K. Murthy for Ayodhya, Sundara and Yuddha. Sanskrit text, translation and translator commentary are different layers. Do not treat a translator's illustrative annex or commentary as a speech in the Sanskrit text.

Uttara uses Hari Prasad Shastri's main numbering 1–111, as presented on Wisdom Library. The lettered supplements 23b–23f, 37b–37f and 59b–59d are explicitly acknowledged. Do not call this exactly 111 entries or transfer its references into Critical Edition numbering.

The Princeton/Goldman introduction is used for textual history and reception. Accessed introduction excerpts and table of contents support the editorial note; no claim is made to have consulted every page of the volume. No modern translation is reproduced wholesale. Narrative prose on RudraNāda is newly written.

## Claims needing particular care

| Claim / distinction | Reference | Treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ahalya's condition is not simply the popular stone image | Bala 48–49 | Identify the passage used; do not claim all traditions agree |
| Rama meets Parashurama after the marriages | Bala 74–76 | Preserve narrative order |
| Sita argues for joining the exile | Ayodhya 26–30 | Preserve her speech and agency |
| Bharata does not engineer the exile | Ayodhya 68–82 | Distinguish absence, discovery and response |
| Sandals express Rama's continuing authority | Ayodhya 112–115 | Distinguish narrative arrangement from later devotional interpretation |
| Sita questions armed intervention | Aranya 9–10 | Include Rama's reply as well as her question |
| Lakshmana Rekha | Aranya 45–49 | Not inserted into this departure scene |
| Shabari's hospitality | Aranya 74 | Berry-tasting detail not inserted |
| Vali challenges Rama | Kishkindha 17–18 | Preserve objection and response |
| Jambavan's reminder | Kishkindha 66–67 | Does not take place in Sundara |
| The actual leap | Sundara 1 | Follows preparation at the end of Kishkindha |
| Sita's reasons for not leaving with Hanuman | Sundara 37–40 | Give multiple reasons rather than a single slogan |
| Nala's bridge | Yuddha 22 | Do not replace collective construction with the inscribed-stone story |
| Hanuman's healing missions | Yuddha 74 and 101 | Do not collapse separate episodes |
| Aditya Hridaya | Yuddha 105 in this presentation | Edition qualification retained |
| Sita's fire ordeal | Yuddha 113–120 | Preserve protest, ordeal, Agni's affirmation and Rama's explanation |
| Rama's return and coronation | Yuddha 121–128 | Festival associations labelled as later living traditions |
| Hanuman's childhood curse | Uttara 36 | Distinguish this explanation from the earlier Jambavan scene |
| Sita's later exile | Uttara 43–49 | Not confused with the fire ordeal |
| Shambuka | Uttara 73–76 | Explicitly explain the text's hierarchy without endorsing it |
| Lava and Kusha at the sacrifice | Uttara 91–94 | Recitation central; no unlabelled import of later horse-capture wars |
| Sita's final departure | Uttara 95–99 | Do not rewrite as return to married palace life |
| Lakshmana and Rama's departures | Uttara 103–111 | Keep the condition, interruption and separation distinct |

## Internal linking

Main guide links to all seven Kandas. Each Kanda links to the overview through breadcrumbs, every Kanda through the directory, adjacent Kandas through previous/next navigation, and contextually relevant existing pages through prose and related reading.

Existing Hanuman article gains contextual Kishkindha and Uttara links. Hanuman, Parashurama, Rama Navami, Hanuman Jayanti, Parashurama Jayanti, Dussehra, Diwali and relevant music pages gain return links. All existing canonical URLs remain in place. New Kandas each have a self canonical and sitemap entry.
