## Source hierarchy and distinctions

1. Sringeri first: located Tattvaloka Vol. 12, part 3 (Aug–Sep 1989), “Rasa Krida,” S. S. N., pp. 52–53. Read page 52 as a rendered scan. It emphasizes love without expected return, Yogamaya, the circle and a caution against imitation. This is a named magazine author's exposition, not a Jagadguru quotation. A second article, “The Autumn Nights,” was located but not used as evidence.
2. Primary scripture: Bhagavata Purana Sanskrit with Bhaktivedanta translation, Book 10, chapters 29–33 on VedaBase. Verified individual verses 10.29.3, 47, 48; 10.30.28; 10.31.4, 9; 10.32.16, 20, 22; 10.33.16, 30, 39. Chapter page verifies 10.33.2–3 and 19 verses in chapter 31.
3. Traditional commentary: Gaudiya interpretation of aradhitah at 10.30.28 attributed to Visvanatha Cakravarti; no claim that all sampradayas share every elaboration. Distinguish Radha's traditional identification from the unnamed gopi in the base narrative.
4. Cross-check: G. V. Tagare's chapter 29 introduction/translation on Wisdom Library for the five-chapter grouping and circular dance.
5. Calendar: Drik Panchang New Delhi 2026 page directly confirms Sunday October 25, Purnima extending into October 26. No universal puja time given. The same source documents Braj's Raas Purnima connection.

Source URLs appear in the article's reader-visible research references. No modern translation copied verbatim; short Sanskrit phrases are original scripture, with brief paraphrased sense.
