## Editorial decisions

- Follow the major traditional life episodes while identifying their source traditions. The later digvijaya literature is not treated as a contemporary travel diary. No claim of an exhaustive daily biography or an unprecedented discovery is made.
- Describe the parents’ prayer and the Shiva avatara tradition as the pre-birth narrative. Do not invent a previous human incarnation.
- Keep Sringeri’s 788–820 CE presentation, modern eighth-century dating discussion, and Kanchi’s 509–477 BCE chronology attributed separately.
- Preserve the Kalady and Chinmaya/Melpazhur Mana birthplace identifications without silently merging the locations.
- Explain sannyasa through the traditional crocodile/consent account and the philosophical context of Mundaka Upanishad 1.2.12. The latter illuminates the commitment; it does not reconstruct a child’s undocumented private thoughts.
- Use Dasha Shloki for the response to Govinda Bhagavatpada in Sringeri’s telling. Do not automatically replace it with the more widely circulated Nirvana Shatkam.
- Keep Aryamba’s consent, the promise, the return and final rites in the main narrative. Summarise the miraculous episodes without inventing historical corroboration.
- Attribute the Mandana–Sureshwara identification to the traditional biography. Preserve Mandana’s independent philosophical contribution.
- Do not repeat conflicting exact childhood ages for Hastamalaka, the geographically inconsistent Mahishmati location in an online abridgement, or an anachronistic identification of the opponent Navagupta with the better-known Abhinavagupta.
- Separate text genres and traditional attribution from scholarly authorship assessment. The article does not assert that every hymn bearing Shankara’s name is historically authenticated.
- Explain jivanmukti and the end of embodied life separately. Keep Kedarnath and Kanchi final-departure traditions distinct.
- Cross-check disciple–peetham assignments. Some institutional summaries swap Padmapada and Hastamalaka; the table follows the commonly transmitted Sureshwara/Sringeri, Padmapada/Puri, Hastamalaka/Dwarka, Totaka/Jyotir scheme, supported by the Divine Life Society account and Puri’s own succession register.
- Show Kanchi alongside the four directional seats while preserving its own foundation and succession account. Avoid implying that a four-versus-five list resolves institutional history.
- Use current institutional notices for Sringeri and Kanchi; Puri’s biography for its leadership; Sringeri’s visit record and a current publisher profile for Dwarka; and the identified lineage’s own website for Jyotirmath. Acknowledge competing Jyotirmath claims without adopting allegations or making a legal determination.
- Treat the 1910 Kalady consecration, the Advaita Sharada publication/digital project, and the 2021 Kedarnath memorial as dated later history. Memorial construction does not resolve competing ancient chronology claims.
